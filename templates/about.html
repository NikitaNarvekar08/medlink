About MedLink

MedLink is a centralized platform that connects patients with pharmacies, blood banks, and blood donors, making essential healthcare resources easier to find, request, and track.

🧑
Patients

Search, reserve and track.

🏥
Pharmacies

Manage stock and reservations.

🩸
Blood Banks

Maintain blood inventory.

🤝
Donors

Respond to blood requests.

Built with Django, MongoDB and Bootstrap as an MCA mini project.
